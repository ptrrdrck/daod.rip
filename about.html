Welcome! Dao Drip is a tool used to study 🔎 translations of the Dàodéjīng (Tao Te Ching). The "drip" button selects a random chapter (out of 81) for you to ponder 💭 and displays the selected translations together. Their likeness and divergence reveal the translator(s) voice 🖍 and the nuance of the original underlying source 🕳.

Your reading history, translation selections and theme are saved locally in your browser — there are no accounts and nothing is sent anywhere. "Clear Local Storage" in settings resets all of it at once. Other settings include translation text size, card layout (grid or stacked), and order (always shuffled or whatever you set it to be). Change the page load setting to either always load a new random chapter or resume from where you left off.

The share button copies a link to the chapter you are on with your current translators attached, so it opens the same way for whoever you send it to. The search looks for a word or phrase in all translations and each result takes you to the chapter and translations it appears in. The library is where you choose which translators appear and in what order, and helps you browse chapters by all, unread or starred.
