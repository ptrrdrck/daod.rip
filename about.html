Welcome! Dao Drip 💧 was made to assist with casual study 🔎 of translations of the Dàodéjīng 📜 (🗣 "Dow Duh Jeang").

The "drip" buttons select a random 🎲 chapter (out of 81) for you to ponder 💭. Your selected translations of the chapter are placed together 🔺. Their differences and similarities reveal the translator(s) voice 🖍 and the nuance of the original underlying source 🕳.

Your reading history, translation selections and theme are saved locally in your browser 🔒 — there are no accounts and nothing is sent anywhere. "Clear Local Storage" in settings resets all of it at once.

The buttons across the top of the reading page: search 🔍 looks for a word or phrase in all ten translations and takes you to the chapter it appears in; the library 📚 is where you choose which translators appear and browse the chapters by all, unread or starred; settings ⚙️ holds the themes 🎨, the text size, the grid or stacked layout, and whether arriving on the site opens a random chapter or the last one you were reading; and share 🔗 copies a link to the chapter you are on with your current translators attached, so it opens the same way for whoever you send it to.

While reading, the star ⭐ keeps a chapter in your starred list, and the arrows above the text step back and forth through the chapters you have already seen. Each translation is quoted in part and credited 📖, with a link on every card to its source — the books themselves are worth owning.
